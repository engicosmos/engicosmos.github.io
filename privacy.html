Privacy policy

EngiCosmos Studio. Effective 9 November 2026.

This policy explains what information EngiCosmos Studio ("the application") accesses, how it is used and how access can be removed. The application is an internal publishing tool operated by the owner of the EngiCosmos YouTube channel and is used only for that channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using the application you are also bound by the Google Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy, and by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Information the application accesses

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google after the channel owner signs in. They allow the application to upload videos to the EngiCosmos channel.
  • The video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags and scheduled publishing times that the channel owner prepares for upload.
  • The IDs of videos the application has uploaded, returned by the YouTube Data API, so the owner can find them.

The application does not access, collect or store personal information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube users, and it does not read data from any channel other than EngiCosmos.

How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the owner's own videos and thumbnails to the EngiCosmos channel and to record which videos were uploaded. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and video data are stored locally on the channel owner's personal computer, inside the encrypted credential store of the automation software the application runs on. They are not uploaded to any server operated by EngiCosmos. Generated video files are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is sent only to Google and YouTube through the YouTube Data API in order to upload videos.

Cookies

The application does not place, access or recognise cookies on users' devices. This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access granted to the application can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked the application can no longer upload videos, and the stored tokens stop working. Stored tokens and upload records are deleted within 7 days of revocation or of a request sent to the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
